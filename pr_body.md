## FocusFlow - Intelligent Local Notification Triage & Filtering

### Overview
FocusFlow is a modern, privacy-focused Android application designed to filter, mute, and triage notifications locally on-device based on context modes (Commute, Focus, Reading, and custom modes).

### UI Walkthrough & Screenshots

#### 1. Main Dashboard & Master Control
- Features a clean, FoxyNotes-inspired hyper-minimalist card layout.
- Includes the **START / STOP** master switch, mode selector, and Muted vs. Shown notification inbox tabs.
